### Foundational Research Declaration

The Dahli Model proposes that dark energy is pure quantized existential energy, distinct from the vacuum energy associated with physical fields.

The framework introduces two types of cosmic vacuum:

1. **Relative Vacuum:** Associated with physical fields and their minimum vacuum energy.
2. **Pure Cosmic Vacuum:** A distinct cosmic structure associated with quantized existential energies and a proposed spatial brane structure.

The model defines pure existential energy as the sum of the pure cosmic energies of all universes, without activation.

Within this total, the single pure cosmic energy that is the exact counterpart of our universe is quantized and distributed through the physical fields as vacuum energy. The remaining existential quanta constitute dark energy.
